Lesson 1: What is Artificial Intelligence?

Learn the basic idea of Artificial Intelligence and understand how AI is used to make computers perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence, commonly called AI, is a technology that allows computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

These tasks can include understanding information, recognizing patterns, solving problems and making decisions.

How Does AI Work?

AI systems use data, algorithms and computer programs to identify patterns and produce useful results.

Simple Example:

If an AI system is given many pictures of cats and dogs, it can learn patterns from those pictures and use them to help identify a new picture.

AI in Everyday Life

We can find AI in many everyday technologies. Examples include voice assistants, recommendation systems, image recognition and chatbots.

Why is AI Important?

AI can help people analyze large amounts of information, automate tasks and solve problems more efficiently.

Lesson Summary 📚

Artificial Intelligence is a technology that enables computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.
